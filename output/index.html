COVID-19 case study: pulse oximetry

A 2020 hospital-cohort study found occult hypoxemia missed by pulse oximetry occurred nearly three times as often in Black patients as White patients. The FDA lists skin pigmentation among factors that can affect readings and later proposed broader performance testing. This is adjacent optical-sensor evidence; it does not imply the ECG dataset or this model has the same disparity. Race is not a direct skin-tone measurement.

Sjoding et al., NEJM 2020 · FDA: Pulse Oximeters · FDA draft performance guidance (2025)
